03 / A way to work together

From a question
to something shared.

A proposed rhythm for chapter activities.
Small steps, with something useful at each one.

  1. 01

    Find a question

    Start with something you want to understand, not a tool you feel you should use.

    Leave with / a clear problem
  2. 02

    Try it together

    Read, prototype, or reproduce a small result. Make room for different skill levels.

    Leave with / a first experiment
  3. 03

    Make it inspectable

    Write down the method. Show your sources, assumptions, and the parts that did not work.

    Leave with / a reviewable artifact
  4. 04

    Pass it forward

    Share the work and what you learned so someone else has an easier starting point.

    Leave with / a useful next step
